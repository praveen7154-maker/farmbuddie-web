Privacy Policy – Irrigo

Farm Buddie Smart Irrigation · Last updated: 7 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Irrigo app (package com.irrigo.app) and the Farm Buddie irrigation controllers collect, why, and how it is handled. Irrigo lets a farmer monitor and control their own pump motor, valves and related equipment.

Information we collect

WhatWhy
Mobile phone numberTo sign you in with a one-time password (OTP) and to check that your number is registered with a Farm Buddie controller.
Name and farm details you enter (farmer name, farm name, controller and unit names)To show and manage your farms in the app.
Equipment data from your controller – motor state, voltage, current, run time, valve states, faults and schedulesTo show live status, send alerts and keep the history used for your reports.
Location – the town you choose for weather, and the GPS position only when you tap to plot a valve on the farm mapFor local weather and to place valves on your farm map. The app does not track your location in the background.
Notification tokenTo deliver alerts (for example a motor fault) to your phone.
Crash and diagnostic reports – device model, Android version, app version and technical details of the errorTo find and fix problems in the app. These reports do not include your phone number or farm data.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera – only to scan the QR code on a controller or unit while pairing. Images are not stored or uploaded.
  • Bluetooth – only to pair new controller units nearby.
  • Location – for plotting valves on the farm map when you choose to (and, on older Android versions, required by the system for Bluetooth pairing).
  • Notifications – to show alerts from your equipment.

How information is used and shared

  • Your information is used only to run the Irrigo service for you.
  • We do not sell your information and do not use it for advertising.
  • It is processed by our service providers only to run the service: Google Firebase (sign-in, database, notifications, crash reporting) and our own cloud server that relays your controller's data.
  • Your dealer/distributor may see the controllers and farms they installed, to provide support.
  • We may disclose information if required by law.

Security

Data between the app, the cloud and your controller is sent over encrypted connections. Controller firmware updates are digitally signed. Saved login details for your farms stay on your phone and are excluded from cloud backups.

Data retention

  • Detailed equipment event history on our server is kept for about 15 days; summary data used for reports may be kept longer.
  • Your account and farm details are kept while you use the service.
  • Crash reports are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days.

Your choices and deleting your data

You can remove a farm from the app at any time. To delete your account and the data linked to your phone number, contact us at the address below; we will delete it within 30 days, except where we must keep it by law.

Children

Irrigo is meant for farmers and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
