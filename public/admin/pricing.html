Price list

📘 How to fill a row (example: Motor Master Hub)
  1. MRP - the printed price including GST, e.g. ₹14,160.
  2. Distributor price - what the distributor pays you, before GST, e.g. ₹10,000.
  3. Farmer selling price - what the farmer normally pays, before GST, e.g. ₹11,000 (farmer pays ₹11,000 + 18% = ₹12,980).
  4. Minimum price - the lowest a distributor may sell at. No discount allowed? Make it the same as the farmer selling price (it fills itself when left empty).
  5. Maximum price - the highest before GST; normally MRP without GST (₹14,160 ÷ 1.18 = ₹12,000). Fills itself from the MRP.
  6. Max discount - worked out for you: Farmer selling price − Minimum price (e.g. ₹11,000 − ₹10,500 = ₹500 discount allowed). Same prices = no discount.
  7. Our cost 🔒 (optional) - what Farm Buddie pays to make/buy one unit, before GST, e.g. ₹7,000. Then Our profit shows: direct sale ₹11,000 − ₹7,000 = ₹4,000 · via distributor ₹10,000 − ₹7,000 = ₹3,000. Only admins see it - never distributors.

Must always be: Distributor price ≤ Minimum ≤ Farmer selling ≤ Maximum, and Maximum + GST ≤ MRP.
