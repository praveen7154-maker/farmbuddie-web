Price list

📘 How to fill a row (example: Motor Master Hub)
  1. MRP - the printed price including GST, e.g. ₹14,160 (₹12,000 without GST).
  2. Distributor price - what the distributor pays you, before GST, e.g. ₹10,000.
  3. Farmer selling price - what the farmer normally pays, before GST, e.g. ₹12,000 (farmer pays ₹12,000 + 18% = ₹14,160). Fills itself from the MRP (MRP without GST) - type a lower one if needed. Distributor margin = ₹12,000 − ₹10,000 = ₹2,000.
  4. Company discount (optional, only for 🏭 Manufacturing products - bought ones get none) - the most Farm Buddie pays per unit, before GST, when the customer asks for a discount, e.g. ₹500. It doesn't change the farmer selling price: the distributor may give any amount up to it, or none. Whatever they give, they pay Farm Buddie that much less (₹10,000 − ₹500 = ₹9,500), so their margin stays ₹2,000. Our profit on that sale drops by the same amount. 0 = none.
  5. Minimum price - the lowest a distributor may go out of their own margin. Nothing from their margin? Make it the same as the farmer selling price (it fills itself when left empty). E.g. ₹11,700 lets them give ₹300 more of their own.
  6. Maximum price - the highest before GST; normally MRP without GST (₹14,160 ÷ 1.18 = ₹12,000). Fills itself from the MRP.
  7. Lowest price - worked out: minimum price − company discount (₹11,700 − ₹500 = ₹11,200). A quote below it can't be saved.
  8. Our cost 🔒 - what Farm Buddie pays to make/buy one unit, before GST, e.g. ₹7,000. It comes from 🏭 Production Pricing when the product's cost is worked out there (then it can't be typed here). Our profit: direct sale ₹12,000 − ₹7,000 = ₹5,000 · via distributor ₹10,000 − ₹7,000 = ₹3,000 (₹2,500 if the full company discount is given). Only admins see it - never distributors.

Must always be: Distributor price ≤ Minimum ≤ Farmer selling ≤ Maximum, Maximum + GST ≤ MRP, and Company discount ≤ Distributor price.

Production pricing - what one unit costs Farm Buddie, before GST 🔒

For each product choose 🛒 Buying - the supplier and the purchase price (plus extra costs like freight, if any) - or 🏭 Manufacturing - every component / material with its cost. The total, without GST, is Our cost in the price lists: draft lists take it at once; a published list keeps the cost it was approved with (use Edit prices to put a new cost in force).
